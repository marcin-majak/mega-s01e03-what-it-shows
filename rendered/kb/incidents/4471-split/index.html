The problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

Day-first tenants (every tenant on file) get day and month swapped when a date goes through the shared parser: app, email, and the booking-v2 form. This is the bug #4471 describes, and the one the queued fix is for.

“Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” (R-41200, harbour.dental, app)

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants on booking-v3 (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Every one of these came through the form. The parser fix does not touch them: the hint, or the form’s reading of it, has to change.

“Customer booked 11 April and got an invite for 4 November.” (R-41259, copper.studio, form)

one-day: the date moves by exactly one day

A month/day swap cannot turn 12 May into 11 May. These reports describe the same booking a day apart, or one day apart for people in different places, and a whole month off by one. That reads like a time zone or day-boundary bug, not a parser one. The desk noted them (oneDayMove in desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant, so the table counts them in both rows.

“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting. We held it twice.” (R-41344, pale.harbour, email)

“Every booking in June is one day off. Only June. Why only June.” (R-41209, alder.physio, app)

How this was counted

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so repeated ids count once (uniqueById).
  • Cause by tenant, not by intake tag: locale and booking form from the tenant directory (causeOf), the way triage explains it.
  • A report in oneDayMove is also one-day, whatever its tenant; it stays counted where the desk files it.
  • The logic is #lib/kb/split.ts; the table is ProblemTable. When reports keep landing on #4471, the counts follow.

Lessons

  • Same subject is not same cause. Count by what the report shows, then merge.
  • A footnote under the biggest row is still a problem. Give it a ticket.
